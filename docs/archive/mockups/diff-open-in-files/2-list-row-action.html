Every row in the changed-files list gains a trailing ↗. It is the literal reading of "on every file": five files, five jumps, all on screen together - so you can open any of them in Files without first making it the file you are reading. It is quiet until you point at a row, and permanent on the row you have selected.

Restructures the row. .diff-fileitem stops being a <button> and becomes a container of two. Nesting them is invalid markup and the inner one is unreachable to a screen reader, so this is not optional.
