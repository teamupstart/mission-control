2/4The Library: one home, framed by the operator's questions

A single new destination (topbar segment, L) gathers everything you author or configure once and reuse - workflows, Personas, ensemble strategies, missions, sources - into shelves. The signature: every shelf is headed by the question it answers ("What counts as done?"), with the system's noun demoted to an eyebrow. Purpose is the information architecture. A "Happening now" rail keeps live runs one click away, healing the launch-here-watch-there split.

01Questions, not nouns. "What counts as done?" explains workflows better than any tooltip. The jargon survives only as a mono eyebrow, so the operator still learns the system's vocabulary.
02One search, everything. The shelf search is the app-wide ⌘K: it indexes workflows, Personas, missions, sources, AND the existing settings controls - the current palette only knows Settings.
03Launch and watch reunited. Strategy cards launch straight into the ensemble dispatch flow; the "Happening now" rail is where those launches land - no more launching in one feature's modal and watching in another feature's fourth tab.
04Cost: the biggest IA change of the four. The Workflows page's tabs dissolve into shelves + detail pages, and Skills / Harnesses / Task sources migrate out of Settings over time (Settings keeps only their consent and blast-radius switches).
