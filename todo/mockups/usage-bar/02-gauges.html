This is what the existing view: "plan" mode is reaching for when the dollars are notional.

Optimises for

Reading three windows as three states without comparing bar lengths. An arc at 91% is unmistakable at any zoom, and the reset time is on the face rather than in a tooltip.

Costs

The tallest of the five - taller than what ships now, so it wants to be the folded-open state rather than the default. Arcs are also poor at comparing two similar values, which bars do for free.

Code it touches

FleetStrip.tsx plus a small arc component. Nothing new from the server: usedPercentage, resetsAt and projectRunway already carry all three faces.

Narrow 940px laptop, or a window at half width

Same bar in the width where the topbar's responsive ladder starts shedding ink. What wraps first, and whether it still reads, is part of the choice.
