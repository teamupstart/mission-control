Guided dispatch

Pressing + asks a short sequence of keyboard questions instead of opening the eight-control form, then hands you the form with every answer already filled in and the cursor in the task box. Four takes, all interactive - open one and drive it with the keyboard. D is the direction being taken forward; A, B and C are the explorations it was synthesized from, kept for the record.

C's in-place choreography with A's rail laid on its side: the form never moves and every picker floats over its own control, while a strip under the header keeps every answer on screen and clickable.
