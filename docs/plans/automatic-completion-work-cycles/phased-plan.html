Repository findings

  1. The prompted guard and incomplete hold are keyed to resolved intent, with an assumption background continuations disprove.
  2. Claude task notifications are correctly filtered from human intent and must remain lifecycle-only signals.
  3. Terminal Stop and SDK turn_done already express the same idle fact, but lack a durable generic identity.
  4. settledIdle remains the shared late-hook and multi-turn ordering guard.
  5. session_events is hook-only by contract because any row currently means hooks were seen.
  6. session_work_episodes owns task, branch, agent, and pull-request provenance rather than individual turns.
  7. Workflow completion claims already retire a Foreman guard atomically with run creation or resumption.
  8. The worker rechecks intent after verification but not idle or completed-turn currency.
  9. Workflow resumption already follows daemon-observed state and evidence; prompted delivery still clears its legacy guard manually.
  10. Tactical task 5439ebd4-e346-4d57-b778-ecb2ebec8e67 is already running and must land or be explicitly superseded first.

Sizing and phase count

420-650estimated non-test implementation lines

The estimate includes shared lifecycle types, harness normalization, additive SQLite state, Registry transitions and projection, prompted selection and consumption, workflow claim changes, cleanup, and documentation.

Why two phases: one change should not simultaneously invent a cross-harness durable lifecycle contract and replace a high-risk shipping trigger after rebasing an in-flight incident fix. Phase 1 is independently testable and behavior-neutral. Phase 2 consumes its fixed contract and performs migration plus cutover together.

Why not three: a separate cleanup phase would leave two prompted trigger identities active in a merged state, which violates the approved replacement decision.
