A trail map for the evening. Off-white stock, a tight sans for anything structural and a serif for prose, forest green against ridge blue. The clearest to read on a phone in a parking lot, and the easiest to fill in quickly.

Five screens at phone width, which is where almost everyone will open the link. Copy, dates and the venue are sample text. Photographs are placeholders - the real wedding album replaces them. Ridge artwork is inline SVG, so it costs nothing to load and scales to any screen.
