Grouped tabs - the column's height becomes the tallest group, not their sum

The smallest change that fixes the length. The stacked cards become four tabbed groups, so the column is as tall as Models rather than as tall as everything. Inside a group nothing is collapsed and nothing takes a second click. The second half of the fix is the prose: a three-line blurb under every dropdown is what turned 14 controls into 1988px, so it moves to a hover and prints in full under the field that has focus.

Whichever group you have open, look at the tab strip. Posture holds one control, Safety two, Launches three, Models five - and Models is the group that made the column long. A tab strip paginates that; it does not shrink it. That is why this option finishes last on the metric that started the exercise, and why it is still worth having on the table: it is the only one of the six that changes no control, no copy and no anchor.

Why this one

  • Smallest change. Cards, fields, anchors, and copy all survive; the panel gains one piece of tab state.
  • Nothing takes a second click once you are in a group - better than A if you are actually tuning something.
  • Reversible. If it reads badly, delete the tab strip and you are back to today with shorter blurbs, which is an improvement on its own.

What it costs

  • You cannot see the tier and the safeguards at once. Tabs hide state, and this panel's state is the point.
  • Still the tallest of the six on its worst tab. Models is five controls and a tab cannot make five dropdowns short.
  • Deep links need a tab to open. The settings search jumping to foreman/skip-scout-wrapup has to switch tabs first, and a tab that switches under you is disorienting unless it flashes.
  • A fifth group makes the strip too narrow to label in words.
