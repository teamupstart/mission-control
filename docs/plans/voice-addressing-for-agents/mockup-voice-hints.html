Inline voice hints

Every surface that accepts a voice response carries a short, always-visible hint in lighter text, with no hover: Say "Steve, option B" to answer on a question card, and one for every action on an inbox card, such as "Steve, mark read", "Steve, nudge" or "reply, ...". An agent with no spoken name is answered by starting with "reply".

Fixed in every variant: the same four cards (Steve's question, Steve's report, Steve's Stuck row, and a question from a session with no spoken name), the phrases the inbox plan declares in INBOX_ACTIONS, and no hint on click-only controls (Open session, Dismiss, the report's show and hide, Mark all read). Chat about it and Remind me later have no declared spoken form, so they carry none. What varies is where the phrases sit and how much of the card they take.
