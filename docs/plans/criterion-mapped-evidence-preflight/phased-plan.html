Why not one phase?

One review would mix atomic evidence storage, structured-model evolution, new concurrent wait states, operator escape paths, built-in workflow versioning, and metric denominator changes.

Why not two?

Keeping analytics out of the enforcement pull request makes the state-machine change reviewable and lets outcome measurement consume its final durable events.

Cross-phase contracts

Evidence identity

Claims and links use caller-stable IDs. Staging generation, reservation, repository scope, and immutable submission capture stay atomic.

Classification authority

Canonical criteria come from Workflow context, stable IDs come from the daemon, hard proof roles come from the author, and semantic sufficiency stays with the Auditor.

Lifecycle accounting

Persona rejection advances a round. Preflight repair advances a segment with evidence_preflight lineage and spends no repair budget. Opaque submission keys support joins, while deterministic event IDs are unique at the shared persistence boundary.

Final verification bar

  • Focused tests always include test/setup-state.mjs.
  • Typecheck, lint, eight-worker unit suite, build, and smoke pass.
  • Built-dashboard Playwright proves non-UI authoring, missing visual proof, preflight wait, same-round repair, override, and analytics.
  • Exact command output and rendered screenshots are registered through Mission Control and remain gitignored.
  • Historical workflow versions and telemetry records remain readable.
