Workflow run detail - four consolidation mockups

Today the run page stacks Repair delivery, Image evidence, Evidence readiness and Captured intent and evidence as four full-width sections, each printing its raw payload inline. On a repair round 6 run that is roughly 11 screens of scrolling below the Review worklist, and the thing an operator actually needs - what state is this in, and what changed - is never on screen at once. Every mockup below keeps all of the same data. They differ in where it goes and what you have to do to reach it.

Tabs under Review worklist

Mockup B

Round record rail (persistent side inspector)

The worklist gets the full column and never moves. The three sections collapse into a docked right rail that shows one at a time, chosen by a segmented control, with its own scrollport. Nothing is hidden behind a click you have to remember to make - the rail is always on screen showing something, and its header always states the round's verdict in a sentence. On a narrow window the rail drops below the worklist and becomes Mockup A's tabs, so there is one behaviour to learn.

Mockup C

One "Round record" section, three summary rows that expand in place

No new navigation at all. The three sections merge into one, and each becomes a single collapsed row whose closed state is a full sentence with the counts and the status chip already in it. Reading the page top to bottom answers the question without opening anything; opening a row expands it in place, exactly where you were. Default state is collapsed, except a row that is blocking - a refused delivery, a readiness block - which opens itself and stays open.

Mockup D

Round dossier above the worklist, raw record in a full-screen reader

The three sections stop being sections. What survives on the page is one three-column dossier above the worklist, carrying only what changes a decision: what this round was trying to do, whether its proof is structurally complete, and whether its packets landed. Every raw payload - repair packets, the original prompt, the image ledger, the snapshot facts - moves out of the page entirely into a full-screen reader with its own left nav, opened from any "open" link. The run page becomes short by construction and can never grow, because raw text no longer has a home on it.

Why this
  • Fixes the actual cause. Raw payloads are what makes the page 11 screens, and they leave it entirely.
  • The dossier reads as one sentence per column - the fastest scan of the four.
  • The reader is a better place to read a 2,000-word packet than a page section ever was: full width, its own nav, keyboard next/previous.
  • The page cannot regrow, because there is nowhere on it to print raw text.
Costs
  • Biggest behavioural change. Everything raw is now two clicks away, and some of it is currently glanced at in place.
  • A new full-screen surface to build, route and keep accessible.
  • The dossier's summaries are derived - each one is a new thing that can be wrong or stale.

All four keep every field that is on the page today. None of them drop a payload, a hash, a timestamp or an action button - the retry, resolve and override controls stay wherever their record goes.
