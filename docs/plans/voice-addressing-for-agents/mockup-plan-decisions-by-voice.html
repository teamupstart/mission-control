Filling a plan-decisions form by voice

A request_plan_decisions form is fill-only by voice: "Steve, first one B, second one A" ticks options and sends nothing. The form waits for "Steve, submit" (or "reply, submit") or a click, and validation refuses to submit a half-filled form.

Fixed in every variant: the filed form as the filed-plan-decisions plan draws it (decisions tag, Revision 2 tag with Earlier version, the gate status line "Waiting on your decisions", no snooze), the agent's display name in its board colour, blue for anything voice filled, and no countdown anywhere, because voice never submits this form on its own. Each decision's Chat about it row and note box are unchanged and left out here to keep the cards short. What varies is how a decision and an option are addressed and how the form shows what is still missing. Each variant shows four moments: at rest, two of three filled, "Steve, submit" refused, and complete.

  • The badge is the spoken grammar printed on the card: “first one B” is badge 1st, letter B. Nothing to translate.
  • A managed plan's final bundle gets an approval decision appended by the daemon, so the ordinals must count it too or “third one” points at the wrong question.
  • A refused submit lands on the decision that caused it, inside the fieldset.decision that DecisionForm already draws.
  • Badges and letters add weight to every decision for an operator who never speaks, and the form looks different with the voice flag off.
