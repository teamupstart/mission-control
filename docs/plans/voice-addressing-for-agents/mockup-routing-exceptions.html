Unsure routing, not running, and waiting behind a question

The confirmation card's other outcomes. When routing is still unsure after the name, phonetic and small-model steps, Mission Control either shows the ambiguity on screen or asks aloud (a user setting). A closed or exited agent gets Steve isn't running with a hint. A session on an older MCP bundle that is blocked on a question cannot be converted, so the message waits and the card says so. A second name in one utterance is either a split or a mention.

These cards take the placement chosen on the confirmation-card page (toast, pill, popover, strip or destination); they are drawn here in a neutral frame. Fixed in every variant: the agent's display name in its board colour, the words as heard, an inline voice hint, and a Recently heard link where a miss can be inspected. What varies is how the operator resolves the doubt and how much Mission Control decides on its own. Each variant shows five moments.
