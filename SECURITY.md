# Security policy

Mission Control is an internal repository. Its security posture, including loopback-only
service access, local ingress authentication, data minimization, and the separately
armed Inspector and YOLO-mode permissions, is documented in [Security](docs/security.md).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities through your team's internal security or engineering
channel. Do not open a public issue or use a public CVE disclosure process for this
repository. Include enough detail to reproduce the concern, but do not include tokens,
credentials, or other secrets in the report.
