3 Repository rulea coloured heading between groups, and the colour on the cards

No frame and no rail. A coloured heading rule separates one repository's cards from the next, and each card in the group carries the repository's colour as the lower third of its own left spine, under the state colour. The colour is on the thing being identified rather than on a container, so a card stays identifiable after its heading has scrolled away. The costs are subtlety - a 3px strip can read as an artifact until you know what it is - and one extra heading, because with no container the cards after a heading belong to it.
