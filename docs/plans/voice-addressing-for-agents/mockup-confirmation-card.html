Confirmation card and cancel window

A routed command plays a soft chime and shows a card: To Steve: check the build. The operator has a few seconds to say "cancel" before it is delivered. After delivery the card reads Sent to Steve. He has two open questions. with the hint Say "Steve, answer" to reply to one, then fades without interrupting.

Fixed in every variant: the agent's display name in its board colour, the message verbatim, a visible countdown, a Cancel control for the mouse, and the inline voice hint. What varies is where the card lives and what the operator sees while Mission Control is minimized. Each variant shows three moments: the cancel window, just after delivery, and Mission Control minimized behind another app.

  • Smallest build: one React toast and the notification path the app already has.
  • macOS decides whether a notification shows (Focus modes, banners off), so the cancel window can pass unseen while minimized.
  • Testable end to end in Playwright, because the card is ordinary dashboard markup.
  • A notification cannot draw a live countdown; it shows the seconds once.

  • One look everywhere, and a real countdown while minimized: the spec's "from anywhere" case is the main case.
  • A second Electron window (frameless, always on top, click-through outside the pill). New shell work, covered by Electron tests and not by Playwright alone.
  • Does not depend on macOS notification settings or Focus modes.
  • One line wide: a long message is truncated in the pill and read in full only in the agent's conversation.

  • Voice has one home: state, confirmation and history all sit under the same icon.
  • The tray is a native menu today (src/main/tray.ts). A custom popover is a new anchored window, and the menu bar's right edge is far from where the operator is looking.
  • Room for the whole message and both buttons, unlike a one-line pill.
  • On a display with a notch or a crowded menu bar the icon, and the popover with it, can be hidden.
