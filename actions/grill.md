# Grill

Interview the human in rounds of decision forms before starting the work, until nothing is left to ask.

## What to do

Use the invoked grill skill to interview the human about the task below before you start it.
Draft, change, or write nothing until the interview is done.

Every round is one `request_plan_decisions` form holding every question that can be asked so
far, each with the option you recommend listed first. Ask at least one round, even when the
request already looks complete, and keep asking until nothing is left to ask. Facts you can
look up yourself are not questions for the human.

## Reading the answers

A round's form may open with nothing selected. An answer is only what the human selected or
wrote: never your recommendation, and never a question they left unanswered. A dismissed
round ends the work, so stop without starting the task.

## When nothing is left to ask

Carry on with the task exactly as it was delivered, and treat the settled decisions as part of
its request. When the task produces a plan or a document, record each decision in it with the
answer the human gave.

## What not to do

- Do not treat a grilling round as the review of anything you deliver. Bind no round to a
  managed plan, and offer no follow-up choices in it that belong to the task's own review.
- Do not ask one question per form, or a question that depends on another still open in the
  same round.
