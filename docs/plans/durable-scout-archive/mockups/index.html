Durable scout archive · phase 3

Scouts

Phases 1 and 2 shipped. Every completed scout already publishes an immutable bundle under ~/.mission-control/scouts, the daemon already indexes and searches them, and scoutsRevision already ticks in the browser. Nothing renders it. These are four takes on the page that should, each driving the same eight fixture archives so they can be compared directly.

They differ on one question: what is the operator actually doing when they come here? Looking something up, reading an answer, browsing what has been learned, or reconstructing a week.

A

Dossier

"I know roughly what I am looking for."

Three panes that never move: a search rail on the left, the report in the middle, and an evidence spine on the right whose stops say at a glance whether an old archive is complete enough to trust. This is the shape the approved plan already describes.

Reading Room

"Just give me the answer, full width."

The archive is a document, so the page is a reading surface. No permanent list at all: search is a ⌘K overlay that opens over the report and closes back into it, and evidence is a filmstrip under the header. The report gets the whole page.

Findings Board

"I do not remember the title. I remember the answer."

Not a list, a board. The largest text on every card is the finding itself, so the page reads as what has been learned rather than what was filed. Browsing beats searching. The report opens as its own full-bleed route.

Research Log

"The one from right after the topbar broke."

Time is the index. One chronological spine with day headers, and the report opens in a drawer over the right side while the log stays readable underneath. The only direction where a scout is visible in the context of what surrounded it.

what all four hold constant

  • The same eight fixture archives, including one partial (the session exited before it submitted a report) and one unreadable (two bundles claim one archive key with different digests). Neither is ever dressed up as complete.
  • The three real index states, ready / partial / unreadable, always paired with a word so colour is never the only carrier.
  • The primary report/report.html rendered in the same sandbox as the Files tab: no scripts, no network, citations as path:line text with no href.
  • Evidence carrying its real vocabulary: role, repo slot, original checkout path, media type, bytes and SHA-256.
  • The absolute bundle directory, copyable, plus Delete scout in btn-danger-ghost from two places.
  • A foreign bundle copied in from another machine, its producer label marked unverified because a manifest claim is not an identity.
