Choosing between them

A · zones in the headerB · state card
Where the move livesHeader action row, position varies with stateAlways the card, same spot every time
Where the reason livesA sentence in the identity block, only when there is oneThe card, always
Header stabilityChanges shape per stateConstant - context plus destructive, always
Vertical cost on a healthy runNoneOne card row saying nothing needs you
Room to explainOne sentence, or a tooltipHeadline plus a sentence, plus round budget
Can point at a section belowIn prose onlyYes, with a scroll-to button
New CSS.wf-run-why, .wf-run-audit.wf-run-state and four children, .wf-run-audit
Overlap with existing bannersNoneSits near .wf-run-notice and .wf-run-stale, which already banner this page - risks three stacked strips
Distance from todaySmall - nothing relocatesLarger - the primary move leaves the header

The honest summary: A is the smaller, safer change and keeps the page dense. B reads better the worse things are going - it treats “what happened and what to do” as one object and has room to say both - at the cost of a permanent card and a real risk of stacking with the two role="status" banners this page already renders on some rounds.
